Memoriae Custodes in Congo · Secretary General

Designing humanitarian projects and carrying an international advocacy effort

Context

For several decades, armed conflicts in eastern Democratic Republic of Congo have caused massive population displacement and a lasting humanitarian crisis.

In response, Memoriae Custodes in Congo works to preserve memory, raise public awareness, and develop initiatives for affected populations. As Secretary General, I contributed to the association's governance, international advocacy efforts, and the design of humanitarian projects.

My role

Secretary General: association governance, international advocacy, and humanitarian project design.

My main contributions

Co-authoring the book Hécatombe congolaise

  • Documenting the realities experienced by civilian populations
  • Contributing to the duty of remembrance and raising awareness among decision-makers and the general public

Designing a humanitarian project

Presented to the European Commission as part of a funding application.

  • Psychological support to help trauma survivors rebuild
  • Health support and financial aid for displaced populations
  • A holistic approach fostering resilience and community rebuilding

International advocacy and awareness-raising

A mission to European institutions in Brussels, followed by launch events in Brussels, Paris, Toulouse and Guadeloupe.

  • Raising European institutions' awareness of the humanitarian consequences of the conflicts
  • Promoting international mobilisation for peace and the protection of civilian populations
