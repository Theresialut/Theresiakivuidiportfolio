Jury, AMOS Business School public speaking competition

Approached via LinkedIn to serve on the jury of the public speaking competition organised by AMOS Business School, at the Gazette Café in Montpellier. Around twenty students in Sports Business and Marketing competed across three rounds: a pitch built around a sporting event, a storytelling exercise about their love of sport, and a "philosophical" round on a sport-related theme.
