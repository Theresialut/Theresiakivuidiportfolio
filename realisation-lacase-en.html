LaCASE Association · Civic Service Volunteer

Supporting international students toward lasting integration

Context

Every year, several hundred international students arrive in France and face numerous challenges: administrative procedures, access to rights, isolation, financial hardship, cultural adaptation and mental health.

Within the LaCASE Association, I helped develop initiatives to support their social integration and independence.

My role

Civic service volunteer, responsible for supporting international students with their administrative and social procedures and their day-to-day integration.

My main contributions

Social and administrative support

  • Supporting more than 200 international students a year with their administrative procedures
  • Referring students to mainstream services (CAF, health insurance, housing, prefecture, etc.)
  • Providing information on social rights and individualised support based on each person's needs

Creating a listening unit

To combat the isolation of international students and prevent psychological hardship, I set up a listening unit offering a space for dialogue, guidance and support. More than 100 students used it. It's probably one of the achievements I'm proudest of, and it directly inspired the creation of a radio programme on these same issues.

  • Breaking isolation and preventing situations of distress
  • Supporting students' wellbeing
  • Strengthening ties with partner organisations

Coordinating a food aid programme

In partnership with Solidarité DOM-TOM. Of the 200 students registered for our support, 50 received food aid over the course of the year.

  • Logistics coordination and partner relations
  • Registering beneficiaries and organising distributions
  • More than 500 parcels distributed, at a unit value of €10.50
  • Weekly updates to the association's website

Communication and events

  • Contributing to the Case Campus radio programme
  • Showcasing the association's work and animating its community
  • Helping organise conferences, round tables and intercultural events

"In and Out of the Box" radio programme

A bi-monthly programme on Radio Campus Montpellier, which I contributed to, born from my experience with the listening unit. It aimed to shine a light on the struggles of minorities, and of students in general, in Montpellier: isolation, discrimination, hardship in all its forms.

Training with partner networks

In connection with the partner networks Animafac and Engagé·e·s et Déterminé·e·s (E&D).

  • Advocacy training with E&D (6 May 2023, Paris): workshop defining advocacy, learning methodologies through case studies, testimonials from advocacy professionals, hands-on practice building one's own strategy, tips for public speaking
  • FOCUS Toulouse (4-5 February 2023)
  • Civic and citizenship training (15-16 December 2022, Bordeaux), with Jeunes Européens France: preparing civic service volunteers for French and European citizenship and civic life

World café and awareness campaign

Facilitating a discussion group at a world café conference on tackling discrimination and mental health prevention. Contributing to the "La pauvreté c'est nulle" ("Poverty sucks") campaign, aimed at deconstructing myths around hardship in general.

What this experience taught me

This experience profoundly changed how I think about support work. I came to understand that meeting a need is not just about offering a solution. It's above all about creating an environment where everyone can regain their bearings, rebuild confidence, and become a full participant in their own journey.
