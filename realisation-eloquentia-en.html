Eloquentia Montpellier · Secretary General

Structuring a public speaking programme for youth empowerment

Context

Public speaking is a powerful lever for empowerment, self-confidence and civic participation. Yet many young people don't always have the spaces or tools they need to express their ideas.

As Secretary General, I contributed to the association's development and to organising activities that help young people build their public speaking skills, critical thinking and self-confidence.

My role

Secretary General, board member: association governance, tracking decisions and supporting the different teams.

My main contributions

Contributing to the association's governance

  • Organising board meetings and writing minutes
  • Tracking collectively made decisions
  • Coordinating the association's activities

Supporting the different teams and the competition's organisation

  • Supporting teams and facilitating information flow
  • Overseeing overall organisation and coordinating with volunteer teams

The essentials

Population reached
Young people, public speaking competition participants
Key skills
Nonprofit governance, coordination, communication
Partners involved
Eloquentia Montpellier's board and volunteer teams
Impact
Supporting youth empowerment through public speaking

Results

  • Taking part in governance as a board member
  • Organising and tracking board meetings
  • Contributing to organising the public speaking competition

Skills mobilised

Nonprofit governance Project coordination Minute-taking Leadership Event organisation

What this experience taught me

This experience introduced me to the responsibilities involved in running an association's governance. It reinforced my conviction that public speaking is a tool for empowerment, fostering civic engagement and a sense of agency.
