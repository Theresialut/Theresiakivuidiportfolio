Génération Égalité programme · Civic Service Volunteer · Peer-led Prevention (LMDE)

Raising high school students' awareness of equality and violence prevention

Context

Gender stereotypes, sexist and sexual violence, and a lack of information on sexual health are major prevention issues from adolescence onward.

As part of the Génération Égalité programme, I took part in awareness-raising sessions with tenth-grade students in several high schools across the Occitanie region, using a participatory approach based on dialogue and peer-led prevention.

My role

Civic service volunteer, peer-led prevention (LMDE): co-facilitating sessions with tenth-grade students in Occitanie.

My main contributions

Training in peer-led prevention

Initial training with our supervisor, followed by a joint training session in Carcassonne bringing together volunteers from several organisations.

  • Better understanding the issues around gender equality
  • Preparing to address sensitive topics with a teenage audience
  • Learning facilitation methods suited to peer-led prevention

Preparing and facilitating sessions

In pairs, across several high schools in Occitanie.

  • Gender equality, gender stereotypes, consent
  • Sexist and sexual violence, contraception, sexual health
  • Creating spaces for dialogue where students could ask questions and share their views

The essentials

Raising awareness from adolescence onward to prevent violence

Results

  • Co-facilitated sessions in several high schools across Occitanie

Skills mobilised

Educational facilitation Prevention Paired facilitation Public speaking Gender equality Participatory teaching

What this experience taught me

This experience taught me that the quality of a session relies as much on its preparation as on its delivery, and that prevention means creating a space of trust in which young people can build their own thinking.
