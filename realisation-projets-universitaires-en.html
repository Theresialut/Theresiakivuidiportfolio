Designing communication strategies in service of the common good

Context

Throughout my university studies in public and political communication, I took part in several group projects applying strategic communication methods to issues of public interest.

My role

Student, applied Bachelor's and Master's projects: designing communication strategies as part of a team.

My main contributions

Developing communication strategies

On topics such as the ecological transition, tackling disinformation, civic participation, international solidarity and public health.

  • Analysing context, stakeholders and target audiences
  • Designing communication plans and awareness campaigns

Projects delivered

  • "Leadership, Politics & Governance" Round Table: organising a round table on tensions between political and administrative power in local government, Palais des Congrès de Béziers, 18 January 2023
  • Communication campaign for the town of Allègre-les-Fumades: a public and political communication campaign plan to showcase the town's heritage and territory
  • Le Grand Oral des Jeunes: a digital public and political campaign project
  • Fake news: analysing and addressing disinformation during the 2022 election period
  • Tous à l'électrique: a digital workshop raising awareness of electric mobility
  • Le Monde sans faim: an awareness campaign on tackling hunger
  • Preventing cyberbullying: an awareness campaign on cyberbullying among young people
  • Preventing smoking: an advertising semiotics project on the dangers of secondhand smoke
  • Analysing communication around the Russo-Ukrainian conflict: a semiotic study of an awareness advertisement about the conflict
  • Branding Sainwell: a branding project for a fashion label
