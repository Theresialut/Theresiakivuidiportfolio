Impact Events MOPA · Project

Mobilising around solidarity through events

Context

Through several solidarity and cultural events, I helped develop initiatives to mobilise citizens around social and humanitarian causes, working both on the ground and in project coordination.

My role

Volunteer contributor: designing, organising and coordinating solidarity and cultural events.

My main contributions

Organising solidarity events and street outreach

  • Designing, organising and coordinating conferences, charity concerts and civic gatherings
  • Organising street outreach for people experiencing homelessness
  • Distributing meals and essential goods

Charity concerts

For the second edition, we partnered with Delivr'aide and Studéa, two Montpellier-based organisations tackling student hardship, to whom the funds raised were donated.

  • More than 600 people gathered in total across the charity concerts
  • €1,250 raised in donations
  • More than 300 parcels distributed

Food distribution and partnerships

  • Taking part in several food distribution operations
  • Identifying and mobilising partners to support the projects

I discovered the power of events when put to the service of a cause: they can create encounters, spark commitment, and sustain mobilisation around a shared project.
