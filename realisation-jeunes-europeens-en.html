Gallery

Photos, posters, articles or certificates related to this project.

European Mobility Week · 13-16 December 2022, Jeunes Européens Bordeaux

What I took away from it: this training, for civic service volunteers, of whom I was one, focused on techniques for engaging with young people. It introduced the "L'Europe par les jeunes" programme and the many opportunities Europe offers young people, along with the concepts of European citizenship and civic engagement. It also included a session dedicated to preparing our own high school sessions.

European Press Days · 13-16 April 2023, Paris

What I took away from it: these round tables made me realise just how fragile the European information space is in the face of disinformation. Journalists and speakers stressed that fact-checking is not just a technical tool, but a condition for democratic trust itself: without rigorous verification of information, European public debate loses its common ground. These discussions also reminded me that European journalism faces specific challenges: the flow of information across countries, languages and cultures, and the absence of a truly shared media space at the continental scale.

Erasmus+ training in Paris · 22-23 February 2023

What I took away from it: the training, held in English, covered the various mobility and engagement schemes across Europe and beyond (VSI, VIE, VIA, Au Pair, Erasmus+, among others), and we designed civic projects aimed at better including young people and raising their awareness of European life, reminding them that they are also European citizens, beyond their nationality. The closing conference, also in English, focused on the accessibility of European elections, a blind spot too often overlooked in the design of election campaigns.
