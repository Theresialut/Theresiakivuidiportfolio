AFEV Montpellier · Volunteer Mentor

Supporting children and teenagers toward educational success

Context

Social and educational inequalities can hold back young people's personal development, self-confidence and academic success.

Through its mentoring programme, AFEV supports equal opportunity by pairing volunteers with young people to offer individualised support, built on listening, trust and consistency. As a volunteer mentor, I supported primary and middle school students.

My role

Volunteer mentor, paired regularly with primary and middle school students, in a relationship of trust built over time.

My main contributions

Individualised support for young people

  • Building their self-confidence and encouraging independence
  • Supporting their motivation at school
  • Fostering their personal development

Supporting educational success

  • Helping organise their schoolwork and developing learning methods
  • Cultural openness and conversations around their aspirations
  • Recognising their progress

Building a relationship of trust

  • Adapting my support to each personality
  • Encouraging students to voice their difficulties and celebrating successes, however small

The essentials

Population reached
Primary and middle school students
Key skills
Mentoring, active listening, teaching methods
Partners involved
AFEV · national mentoring programme
Impact
Contributing to equal opportunity through human, personalised support

Results

  • Regular support for primary and middle school students
  • Building relationships of trust that foster young people's engagement
  • Contributing to a national programme for equal opportunity

Skills mobilised

Mentoring Educational support Active listening Teaching methods Building independence Kindness

What this experience taught me

This experience taught me that educational support goes far beyond academic help. By building a relationship of trust and recognising progress, it's possible to make a lasting contribution to a young person's personal development and educational path.
