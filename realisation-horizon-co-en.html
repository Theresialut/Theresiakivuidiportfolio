Horizon.co · Consultant, inclusion, public communication and social innovation

Turning field experience into support and training solutions

Context

After several years working with young people, international students, nonprofits and inclusion stakeholders, I wanted to create a framework that would let me put that experience to work for organisations.

That's how Horizon.co came about: a consulting and support organisation dedicated to the development of people, organisations and social impact projects. It's built on a simple conviction: reveal talent, build resilience.

My role

Founder and consultant: defining the positioning, offering and support methodology.

My main contributions

Designing a support offering

Built around several areas of intervention.

  • Inclusion and diversity, strategic communication
  • Skills development, inclusive leadership
  • Project support, career guidance and professional integration

Building a project ecosystem

  • "Ma Voix, Ma Voie" ("My Voice, My Path"), a mentoring programme for young people's career guidance and integration
  • Awareness, training and public speaking initiatives around inclusion

Communication strategy

  • Creating the visual identity and the website
  • Editorial strategy for social media
